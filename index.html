🚀 Multi-Buy Engine

How do I pick strategies and place stakes? +
Tap any strategy chip in the Strategy Switcher — it highlights solid green with a ✓. A row appears in Active strategies (#1 — Only Ups · Stake [ ]) and the stake box opens ready for input. Tap up to 3 chips; each keeps its own stake. Tap again to un-highlight and remove its row.
What happens when I press Run? +
Every highlighted strategy buys its contract at the same time with its own stake, then the engine loops round-after-round (thread-style) until your Rounds target, Take Profit or Stop Loss.
What analysis engine powers the bot? +
The EMRON PULSE engines: Rise/Fall (momentum+structure+trend+regime), Wilson-z digit tests (Even/Odd, Over/Under), staged Dollar Printer Matches, barrier-digit High/Low (Rise = / Fall =), and run-probability Only Ups/Downs. With Signal gating ON, a leg only fires when its engine currently qualifies.
