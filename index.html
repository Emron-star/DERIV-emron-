🚀 Video Concurrent Execution Strategy

What is the Even/Odd strategy? +
Predicts whether the last digit of the next tick will be even (0,2,4,6,8) or odd (1,3,5,7,9). It uses AI to detect parity patterns and trades only when the model has a statistical edge.
What is Only Ups / Only Downs? +
Only Ups (RUNHIGH) pays if the exit price is higher than entry. Only Downs (RUNLOW) pays if the exit price is lower. The video engine submits RUNHIGH (Only Ups) and RUNLOW (Only Downs) as two separate contracts concurrently. Each uses its own stake. Tick duration is user selectable.
Why does the bot place both sides at once? +
The dual-contract engine fires both contract proposals in the same microtask (no queue, no round-trip delay). This guarantees maximum speed and — on strict 50/50 balance — hedges the position so that the payout from one leg covers the stake on the other.
Why 2 ticks for Ups/Downs? +
A single tick is too noisy for directional strategies. Two ticks smooths out noise while remaining ultra-fast. You can change it in Quick Settings or the Ups/Downs config.

📊 Trading & Signals

How are AI signals generated? +
Signals are generated by a parity-focused ensemble (Even/Odd) and a direction-focused ensemble (Ups/Downs), both using Markov transition models, run-length analysis, and entropy scoring.
What markets are supported? +
All Deriv synthetic volatility indices: Volatility 10/25/50/75/100 (2-second and 1-second ticks), plus Crash, Boom, and Range Break.

📦 Bulk Purchase

What is Bulk Purchase? +
Buy the same options contract for up to 100 end-user accounts in a single call. Authentication is per-account via PAT tokens.
Do I need OAuth for Bulk Purchase? +
No. The endpoint authenticates with your application's Deriv-App-ID header plus the per-account PATs in the request body.
