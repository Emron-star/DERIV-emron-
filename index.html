DigitEdge is our AI-powered signal engine that analyzes synthetic indices in real time and predicts the last digit of the next tick. It's now completely free — no subscription required.

Click "Activate" on the Trading Hub, set your stake, and let DigitEdge do the rest. The bot will automatically place DIGITOVER contracts on every tick with a 90% theoretical win rate on "Over 0".
Is DigitEdge really free? +
