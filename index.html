🎯 Strategy Selection

How do I select strategies?+
At the top of the Trading Hub you'll find a horizontal row of strategy chips (Even, Odd, Matches, Differs, Over, Under, Rise, Fall, Only Ups, Only Downs, High Tick, Low Tick). Tap a chip to add it to the active list. Tap again to remove it. The order you add them is used by Sequential Recovery. All Selected Together evaluates every selected strategy on the active market, while AI Best Confidence ranks the selected strategies across the live markets.
What does each strategy need?+
Most strategies only need a stake and ticks. Over, Under, Matches, Differs, High Tick and Low Tick also use a "prediction" field (a digit 0–9). For High Tick and Low Tick that digit is the barrier — the next tick's last digit must land above it (High Tick) or below it (Low Tick).
How does switch-on-loss and adaptive survival work?+
A loss updates EMRON's learned outcome model. The next entry can switch strategy and market, raises the required confidence when the account is under pressure, reduces stake during drawdown, and refuses to force an entry when no selected strategy/market clears the adaptive gate. The configured emergency stop-loss and account survival floor remain hard limits.
What are High Tick and Low Tick?+
High Tick (TICKHIGH) pays when the last digit of the next tick is higher than your barrier digit. Low Tick (TICKLOW) pays when it's lower. Both are single-tick contracts and use the same Pulse "highlow" engine — a Wilson-adjusted proportion test comparing the observed above/below distribution against the fair baseline for your barrier.

📡 EMRON Pulse

What signals does Pulse produce?+
Pulse runs six engines in parallel: Rise/Fall (price momentum + structure + regime), Even/Odd (digit parity), Over/Under (digit threshold), Matches (Dollar Printer staged rules), High/Low Tick (barrier digit), and Only Ups/Downs (run-length probability).
How does the bot pick a signal?+
For the current strategy in the cycle, the bot asks Pulse for that strategy's confirmation on the selected market. If the signal is active, it places the contract. Otherwise it waits for the next tick.

🎯 AI Hunter

What does the Sniper Hunter scan?+
Sniper Hunter scans all 15 markets (10 Volatility + 5 Jump) across four signal families: Rise/Fall (momentum + structure + trend + efficiency ratio), Only Ups / Only Downs (2-tick run-length probability over the last 50 ticks), High/Low Tick (barrier-digit z-test against the fair baseline), and Over/Under (barrier z-test). Each tab re-ranks every market and only shows the strongest edges at or above the active threshold.
How does the High / Low Tick tab pick a barrier?+
The High/Low Tick engine tests every barrier from 1 to 8 on each market and returns the strongest above/below deviation. The winning barrier is carried into the contract, so the trades fire with the exact barrier the AI selected, not a hardcoded default.
Why does Only Ups/Downs use 2 ticks?+
Only Ups/Downs contracts pay when the market moves in the same direction for N consecutive ticks. EMRON is calibrated for a 2-tick duration — the confidence model uses a baseline of 0.25 (a 2-in-a-row directional bias) rather than the much rarer 5-in-a-row baseline, giving more realistic and actionable confidence scores.
Can I still trade Over/Under and Even/Odd from the Hunter?+
Yes. The Even/Odd tab carries the parity scan, and the O1/U8, O2/U7 and O3/U6 tabs carry the barrier scans. Use the signal tab row to switch between them at any time.

🧠 Adaptive Survival Brain

What does self-learning do?+
Each settled EMRON trade is recorded against strategy, market and detected regime. Over time the model estimates a learned win rate and reward history, then raises or lowers the entry bar for that context. Learning data is stored locally in this browser; the PAT is not stored in the learning model.
What happens when a market stops meeting its threshold?+
With Auto Market Switch enabled, EMRON evaluates the selected strategies across the live market set and can move to another market that clears both the live signal threshold and the adaptive learning gate. It can also rotate strategy during recovery.
How does the $1,000 target and survival lock work?+
The daily EMRON target defaults to $1,000 of recorded EMRON profit for the current account/day. Reaching it stops the bot. A balance at or below the configured account floor, or a recorded floor breach, locks the bot for that account/day. The $1,000 target is a goal, not a guaranteed result.
Does profitable demo trading automatically switch to REAL?+
No. EMRON can reach a LIVE-READY validation state after sufficient positive training evidence, but the REAL mode remains a manual human decision.
