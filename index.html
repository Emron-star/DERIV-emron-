FAQ

 

Is this affiliated with Deriv?

No. It is an independent tool that talks to Deriv's public API.

Is my login safe?

You provide a token you create and can revoke at any time. It is held in session storage and discarded when the tab closes.

Does the bot make money?

No strategy here has a proven edge. Payouts price in a house margin, so expect losses over time. Test on demo.

Can I use a demo account?

Yes, and it is the default.
